MIT 8103 — Advanced Database Systems
Portfolio 1: Database Design and Modelling
Section 6: Functional Requirements
6.1 Introduction

The functional requirements define the operations that the CloudMart E-Commerce Management System database must support.

These requirements are derived from the proposed business processes, intended users, and identified data requirements.

They will guide the subsequent definition of business rules, entities, attributes, relationships, and database constraints.

6.2 Customer Management

The system shall support customer registration, retrieval, and authorised modification of customer information. It shall also support the management of addresses associated with customers.

6.3 Product and Category Management

The system shall support product registration, retrieval, and authorised updates. It shall also support the creation and maintenance of product categories and the association of products with their appropriate categories.

6.4 Inventory Management

The system shall maintain inventory quantities and support authorised inventory adjustments. It shall provide access to current stock information and support inventory changes associated with order processing.

6.5 Order Management

The system shall support the creation and retrieval of customer orders. Each order shall be associated with the relevant customer and shall support multiple order items.

The system shall maintain the product, quantity, and applicable unit price associated with each order item. It shall also support the maintenance and retrieval of order statuses.

6.6 Payment Management

The system shall maintain payment records associated with orders. Payment records shall contain the information required to identify the transaction, amount, payment method, status, and relevant transaction reference.

The system shall support authorised retrieval of payment information and reconciliation of payments with their associated orders.

6.7 Delivery Management

The system shall maintain delivery records associated with orders. It shall support the recording and retrieval of tracking information and delivery statuses, as well as authorised updates to delivery progress.

6.8 Reporting and Information Retrieval

The system shall support the retrieval of customer order histories, sales information, and inventory information.

It shall also support queries that retrieve related information from multiple data categories.

6.9 Requirement Validation

The functional requirements will be validated during implementation through appropriate database operations, test data, SQL queries, and recorded results.

Requirements involving inventory consistency, payment processing, concurrent transactions, and data integrity will be examined further when defining the business rules and transaction controls.

6.10 Summary

The functional requirements establish the operations that the CloudMart database must support.

They provide the basis for defining the system's business rules and developing a database model that accurately represents the organisation's operational needs.
