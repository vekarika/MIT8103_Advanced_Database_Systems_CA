Section 10: Relationship and Cardinality Analysis
10.1 Introduction

Relationship analysis establishes how the identified CloudMart entities are associated and defines the cardinality of each relationship.

The relationships are derived from the case study requirements and business rules. They will guide the construction of the ER diagram and relational schema.

10.2 Customer and Order

One customer may place multiple orders, while each order belongs to one customer. This is a one-to-many relationship implemented through a customer foreign key in the Order entity.

10.3 Customer and Address

One customer may maintain multiple saved addresses, while each saved address belongs to one customer. This is a one-to-many relationship implemented through a customer foreign key in Address.

The design must separately account for preserving the address applicable to an order.

10.4 Category and Product

One category may contain multiple products, while each product belongs to one category under the initial design assumptions. This is a one-to-many relationship implemented through a category foreign key in Product.

10.5 Product and Inventory

Under the initial single-location design, each product may have zero or one inventory record, while each inventory record must reference exactly one product. This is an optional one-to-one relationship from Product to Inventory. A UNIQUE constraint on Inventory.product_id prevents multiple inventory records for the same product.

The database does not require every product to have an inventory record immediately. CloudMart may register a product before its stock record is configured. Business logic should prevent a product from becoming available for sale until inventory is configured.

10.6 Order and OrderItem

One order may contain multiple order items, and each order item belongs to one order. This is a one-to-many relationship implemented through an order foreign key in OrderItem.

A submitted order must contain at least one item. This minimum participation rule requires enforcement beyond a basic foreign key.

10.7 Product and OrderItem

One product may appear in multiple order items, while each order item references one product. This is a one-to-many relationship implemented through a product foreign key in OrderItem.

The OrderItem entity resolves the many-to-many relationship between Order and Product.

10.8 Order and Payment

One order may have multiple payment records, while each payment record belongs to one order. This is a one-to-many relationship implemented through an order foreign key in Payment.

Payment status must be considered when reconciling payments with orders.

10.9 Order and Delivery

One order may have multiple delivery records under the initial split-delivery assumption, while each delivery record belongs to one order. This is a one-to-many relationship implemented through an order foreign key in Delivery.

10.10 Summary

Eight relationships have been identified for the initial CloudMart conceptual model. Their cardinalities and foreign key locations provide the foundation for the ER diagram and relational schema.

The final design will be validated against the business rules and refined where necessary.
