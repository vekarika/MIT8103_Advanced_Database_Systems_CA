CLOUDMART E-COMMERCE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM
RELATIONAL SCHEMA DESIGN

1. INTRODUCTION

The relational schema translates the CloudMart Entity-Relationship Diagram
into a collection of related tables. Each table represents an entity, and
primary and foreign keys establish relationships between the tables.

The design uses nine tables:
Customer, Address, Category, Product, Inventory, Customer_Order,
Order_Item, Payment, and Delivery.

2. RELATIONAL SCHEMA NOTATION

PK = Primary Key
FK = Foreign Key
UK = Unique Key or UNIQUE constraint
NN = NOT NULL constraint

A primary key uniquely identifies each record in a table.
A foreign key references a key in another table and helps maintain
referential integrity.
A unique constraint prevents duplicate values in the specified column
or combination of columns.
A NOT NULL constraint prevents a column from containing NULL.

3. TABLE DEFINITIONS

3.1 CUSTOMER

Customer(
    customer_id PK,
    first_name,
    last_name,
    email UK,
    phone,
    password_hash,
    created_at,
    status
)

Primary Key:
customer_id

Unique Constraint:
email

Purpose:
Stores customer account and contact information.

3.2 ADDRESS

Address(
    address_id PK,
    customer_id FK,
    address_line,
    city,
    state_region,
    postal_code,
    country,
    address_type
)

Primary Key:
address_id

Foreign Key:
customer_id references Customer(customer_id)

Purpose:
Stores customer delivery addresses. A customer may maintain multiple
addresses, and each address belongs to one customer.

3.3 CATEGORY

Category(
    category_id PK,
    category_name,
    description
)

Primary Key:
category_id

Purpose:
Organises products into categories.

3.4 PRODUCT

Product(
    product_id PK,
    category_id FK,
    product_name,
    description,
    selling_price,
    status,
    created_at
)

Primary Key:
product_id

Foreign Key:
category_id references Category(category_id)

Purpose:
Stores product information and its associated category.

3.5 INVENTORY

Inventory(
    inventory_id PK,
    product_id FK, UK,
    quantity_available,
    reorder_level,
    last_updated
)

Primary Key:
inventory_id

Foreign Key:
product_id references Product(product_id)

Unique Constraint:
product_id

Purpose:
Stores the available quantity and reorder level for each product.

Design Assumption:
CloudMart initially operates a single central inventory location.
Each product must have exactly one inventory record.

3.6 CUSTOMER_ORDER

Customer_Order(
    order_id PK,
    customer_id FK,
    delivery_address_id FK,
    order_date,
    order_status
)

Primary Key:
order_id

Foreign Keys:
customer_id references Customer(customer_id)
delivery_address_id references Address(address_id)

Purpose:
Stores order information, the customer who placed the order, and the
selected delivery address.

Design Note:
The delivery address must belong to the customer who placed the order.
This ownership rule requires additional integrity enforcement beyond
the two foreign keys listed above.

3.7 ORDER_ITEM

Order_Item(
    order_item_id PK,
    order_id FK,
    product_id FK,
    quantity,
    unit_price
)

Primary Key:
order_item_id

Foreign Keys:
order_id references Customer_Order(order_id)
product_id references Product(product_id)

Purpose:
Stores individual products included in an order, their quantities,
and the unit prices recorded for the order.

Derived Value:
Line subtotal = quantity * unit_price

Design Note:
Unit price is stored in Order_Item to preserve the price applicable
to the purchase, even if the current Product selling price changes.

3.8 PAYMENT

Payment(
    payment_id PK,
    order_id FK,
    amount,
    payment_method,
    transaction_reference,
    payment_status,
    payment_date
)

Primary Key:
payment_id

Foreign Key:
order_id references Customer_Order(order_id)

Purpose:
Records payment attempts and payment information associated with
an order.

Design Note:
An order may have multiple payment records, for example when an
initial payment attempt fails and a later attempt succeeds.
A payment record does not automatically mean that an order is paid.

3.9 DELIVERY

Delivery(
    delivery_id PK,
    order_id FK,
    tracking_number,
    delivery_status,
    dispatch_date,
    delivery_date
)

Primary Key:
delivery_id

Foreign Key:
order_id references Customer_Order(order_id)

Purpose:
Records delivery and tracking information associated with an order.

Design Note:
An order may have multiple delivery records to support split
deliveries under the initial business assumptions.

4. DERIVED VALUES

Two values are treated as derived rather than independently maintained
source values.

Line Subtotal:
quantity * unit_price

Order Total:
The sum of the applicable order-item subtotals.

These values can be calculated through queries or controlled database
logic. If the implementation later stores them for performance reasons,
the database must ensure that stored values remain consistent with
their underlying records.

The initial model excludes separate calculations for taxes, shipping
charges, and discounts.

5. REFERENTIAL INTEGRITY

The following foreign-key relationships must be maintained:

1. Address.customer_id -> Customer.customer_id
2. Product.category_id -> Category.category_id
3. Inventory.product_id -> Product.product_id
4. Customer_Order.customer_id -> Customer.customer_id
5. Customer_Order.delivery_address_id -> Address.address_id
6. Order_Item.order_id -> Customer_Order.order_id
7. Order_Item.product_id -> Product.product_id
8. Payment.order_id -> Customer_Order.order_id
9. Delivery.order_id -> Customer_Order.order_id

Foreign keys prevent records from referencing nonexistent parent
records. Additional constraints or transaction logic may be required
to enforce business rules that involve multiple tables.

6. DESIGN ASSUMPTIONS

1. Each customer has a unique email address.
2. Each product belongs to one category.
3. Each product has exactly one inventory record in the initial model.
4. An order belongs to one customer.
5. Each order references one delivery address belonging to that customer.
6. An order contains one or more order items when submitted.
7. An order may have multiple payment records.
8. An order may have multiple delivery records.
9. Unit price is recorded in Order_Item to preserve the purchase price.
10. Line subtotals and order totals are derived values.
11. Discounts, taxes, shipping charges, and multiple inventory locations
    are outside the initial model.

7. CONCLUSION

The CloudMart relational schema translates the ER diagram into nine
related tables. Primary keys identify records, foreign keys establish
relationships, and unique constraints support data integrity.

Further work will define the exact database data types, NOT NULL rules,
CHECK constraints, deletion and update behaviour, and additional
mechanisms required to enforce business rules.
