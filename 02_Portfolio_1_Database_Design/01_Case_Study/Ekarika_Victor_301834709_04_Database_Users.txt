MIT 8103 — Advanced Database Systems
Portfolio 1: Database Design and Modelling
Section 4: Database Users and Responsibilities
4.1 Introduction

The CloudMart E-Commerce Management System will support several categories of users, each with different operational responsibilities and information requirements.

Identifying these users is necessary to understand how the database will be accessed, what operations it must support, and which information each role should be authorised to access.

The proposed user groups include customers, sales and order staff, inventory managers, finance and payment staff, delivery staff, system administrators, and management.

4.2 Customer

Customers interact with the e-commerce platform to browse available products, maintain their account information, place orders, and review their order histories.

The database must support the retrieval of product information and authorised access to customer-specific records.

Customer access should be restricted to information they are authorised to view or modify.

4.3 Sales and Order Staff

Sales and order staff are responsible for reviewing and managing customer orders.

Their activities include retrieving order information, reviewing associated order items, and performing authorised order-status updates.

The database must maintain the relationships between customers, orders, and order items to support these operations.

4.4 Inventory Manager

The inventory manager maintains product information and monitors stock quantities.

The database must support product creation and updates, category assignment, inventory retrieval, and authorised stock adjustments.

Inventory updates must preserve data integrity and account for concurrent operations where applicable.

4.5 Finance and Payment Staff

Finance and payment staff review payment records and reconcile payments with customer orders.

The database must support the retrieval of payment amounts, transaction references, payment methods, and payment statuses.

Payment records must be associated with the relevant orders, and updates must follow appropriate authorisation and transaction-control rules.

4.6 Delivery Staff

Delivery staff access delivery assignments and update delivery progress.

The database must support the retrieval of delivery details, tracking references, and delivery statuses.

Delivery information must be linked to the appropriate orders to support order fulfilment and tracking.

4.7 System Administrator

The system administrator manages the technical administration of the database.

Responsibilities include managing database accounts, assigning appropriate permissions, maintaining configuration, and supporting database operations.

Administrative privileges must be controlled to reduce the risk of unauthorised access or modification.

4.8 Management

Management requires access to authorised operational reports to support business decisions.

These reports may include sales summaries, product performance, inventory levels, and order statistics.

The database should support efficient retrieval and aggregation of relevant information.

4.9 Access-Control Considerations

The database should apply the principle of least privilege, under which each user receives only the permissions necessary to perform their assigned responsibilities.

Customer information and payment records should be protected from unauthorised access.

Administrative operations should be restricted to appropriately authorised users.

The final database implementation will determine how these requirements are enforced through database permissions and any application-level controls.

4.10 Summary

The identification of CloudMart's database users establishes the operational and access requirements that will guide subsequent database design.

These user responsibilities will inform the data requirements, functional requirements, business rules, and security considerations developed in the following stages of Portfolio 1.
